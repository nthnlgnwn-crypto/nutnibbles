What this is

Not a generic influencer page.

It works best with a real story: a meal worth explaining, a hotel stay with practical notes, a local experience worth describing, a place people can plan around.

It’s personal, photo-heavy and written for readers. I’m not trying to make everything sound perfect, just clear, useful and honest.

Best Fit

Food

Travel

Local

The rules

Trust first, polish second.

  • Paid or hosted work gets disclosed. Clearly.
  • I say what worked and what didn’t.
  • No fake reviews, inflated claims, or guaranteed positive writeups.
  • Readers should leave with useful context, not just pretty photos.
