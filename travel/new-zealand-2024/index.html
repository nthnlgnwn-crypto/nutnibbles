New Zealand / 20-25 Jul 2024

Steam, road clouds, and green hills in one soft winter loop.

This New Zealand trip felt like a compact road journal: arriving through Auckland, moving into Rotorua and Taupo mist, catching Taranaki from New Plymouth, then ending with the gentle storybook green of Matamata and a Hamilton pause.

It was not a loud trip. The whole route works better as atmosphere than spectacle, which is probably why it keeps lingering in memory: steam, road weather, reflective buildings, calm water, and unexpectedly bright hills at the end.

Route

Auckland start, then the North Island slowly opens.

The photo trail begins around Auckland and Takanini, then follows Rotorua, Taupo, New Plymouth, Hamilton, and Matamata across a short July winter route that feels bigger in mood than in distance.

Vibe

Moody weather, bright greens, small road-trip pauses.

The charm is in the contrast: cloudy lakes, geothermal steam, quiet views, mirrored city surfaces, and bright green hills that suddenly make the whole route feel lighter near the end.

Rotorua and Taupo

Steam and lake weather

The first proper chapter is grey, damp, and atmospheric, with geothermal textures and Lake Taupo under heavy clouds.

New Plymouth

Taranaki and shiny city corners

This was the visually strongest stop: mountain views, lake paths, a sunset edge, and a reflective city building that felt almost futuristic.

Matamata and Hamilton

Green hills and round doors

The closing chapter leans softer: hillside paths, round-door details, and the kind of place that makes the camera slow down.

Original Itinerary

How the winter loop was paced

Best way to use this: the planner for this trip was very road-led, so I turned it into a cleaner route rhythm instead of dropping in the timetable exactly as it was.

19-20 Jul / Flights and Rotorua arrival

The trip began in transit, then settled into steam-country

This opening was mostly about getting into the right mood: long flights, a drive out, and one first proper dinner after landing.

  • CGK to Sydney, then Sydney to Auckland before the road south began.
  • Drive into Rotorua as the first real base for the trip.
  • Eat Streat and Atticus Finch as the welcome-into-winter meal rhythm.
21-22 Jul / Rotorua, Taupo, and the long westward move

Steam, sulfur, falls, and a lot of weather

This was the most obviously “New Zealand” stretch on paper: geothermal stops, cloud-heavy lake views, and long scenic driving.

  • Skyline Rotorua, Stratosfare, and Hell’s Gate Geothermal Reserve.
  • Wai-O-Tapu Thermal Wonderland, Huka Falls Lookout, and Lake Taupo under grey skies.
  • Long drive onward to New Plymouth, with Social Kitchen waiting at the end.
23-25 Jul / Taranaki to Hamilton

The most photogenic middle stretch

This is where the trip started feeling more distinctive: cleaner mountain views, stronger visual stops, then a softer inland finish.

  • Te Rewa Rewa Bridge and Lake Mangamahoe for the Taranaki mood.
  • Govett-Brewster Art Gallery / Len Lye Centre and Shining Peak Brewing in New Plymouth.
  • Waitomo Glowworm Cave, Hamilton Gardens, and the Hobbiton Movie Set Tour.
26-28 Jul / Auckland finish and flight home

A lighter final chapter before the long way back

The road trip ended with city energy again, but it stayed easy and a little playful right up to the flight home.

  • Drive into Auckland, then Queen Street, Khu Khu, and Gilt Brasserie.
  • Sky Tower and a more adventurous last full day around the city.
  • Auckland to Sydney, then Sydney back to Jakarta.
Rotorua & Taupo New Plymouth Matamata & Hamilton

Best way to read this trip: go in order. Rotorua and Taupo set the weather and mood, New Plymouth gives the clearest visuals, and Matamata closes the route on the softest note.
