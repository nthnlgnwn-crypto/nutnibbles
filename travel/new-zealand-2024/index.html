New Zealand / 20-25 Jul 2024

Steam, road clouds, and green hills in one soft winter loop.

This New Zealand trip felt like a compact road journal: arriving through Auckland, moving into Rotorua and Taupo mist, catching Taranaki from New Plymouth, then ending with the gentle storybook green of Matamata and a Hamilton pause. It was not a loud trip, more like a sequence of soft views that became better in memory.

Route

Auckland start, then the North Island slowly opens.

The photo trail begins around Auckland and Takanini, then follows Rotorua, Taupo, New Plymouth, Hamilton, and Matamata across a short July winter route.

Vibe

Moody weather, bright greens, small road-trip pauses.

The charm is in the contrast: cloudy lakes, geothermal steam, quiet views, mirrored city surfaces, and bright green hills that suddenly make the whole route feel lighter.

Rotorua and Taupo

Steam and lake weather

The first proper chapter is grey, damp, and atmospheric, with geothermal textures and Lake Taupo under heavy clouds.

New Plymouth

Taranaki and shiny city corners

This was the visually strongest stop: mountain views, lake paths, a sunset edge, and a reflective city building that felt almost futuristic.

Matamata and Hamilton

Green hills and round doors

The closing chapter leans softer: hillside paths, round-door details, and the kind of place that makes the camera slow down.
