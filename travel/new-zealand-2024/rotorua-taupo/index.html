New Zealand / Rotorua and Taupo / Jul 2024

Rotorua and Taupo under grey skies

First stop out of Auckland. Steaming ground, damp weather, cold lake views. This is where it started to feel like a road trip.

  • Rotorua + Taupo
  • North Island
  • Winter drive
  • Geothermal + lake
  • Grey skies

This is where it started feeling like New Zealand. Steam, wet roads, heavy clouds, and water that looked colder than I expected.

Not sunny at all. I remember it better because of that.

No giant highlight needed. Grey light, steam coming out of the rocks, rain by the lake, and a slower pace after arriving.

Good first stop if you want the trip to start with weather and landscape, and take it slow.
