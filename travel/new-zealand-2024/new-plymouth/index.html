New Zealand / New Plymouth / Jul 2024

Taranaki and New Plymouth

My best photos of the trip are from here. Mountain views, calm lake paths, a shiny mirrored building, a sunset, and one quiet dinner.

  • New Plymouth
  • Taranaki view
  • Lake paths

Not a lot of activities. Just a lot of different things to look at: mountain, water, a mirror building, evening light, and one simple dinner.

After all the grey steam and lake weather, New Plymouth was clear, open, and easy to photograph.

The Taranaki view was the main event. Clear winter day, which is what I always hope for on a road trip. Bright sky, calm water, the mountain just sitting there in the back.

Plus some variety. Same stop: lake and mountain, then shiny city buildings, sunset, dinner.
