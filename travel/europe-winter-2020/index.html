Europe / 31 Dec 2019 - 12 Jan 2020

Europe Winter 2020

A fast winter run from Milan into Switzerland, through Germany, Amsterdam and Brussels, ending in Paris. Switzerland and Paris are the parts I remember best.

  • Europe

  • Milan -> Paris route
  • Winter road + train trip
  • Balanced journal

Beautiful route, but I moved way too fast. New city every one or two nights, plus road time, trains and luggage. Full, expensive, and sometimes tiring.

Europe in winter, yes. But mostly I learned how much moving around a trip can take before the luggage starts winning over the views.

Jakarta to Milan, then Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken, Montreux, Bern, Frankfurt, Koln, Amsterdam, Brussels and Paris, then home.

Switzerland and Paris are still the best parts. Switzerland was extra expensive, but the winter views made it worth it. Paris was an easier ending: city lights, macarons, and one proper lunch at Kong.

At a Glance

  • RouteJakarta -> Milan -> Zurich -> Lucerne -> Interlaken -> Montreux -> Bern -> Frankfurt -> Koln -> Amsterdam -> Brussels -> Paris -> Jakarta.
  • Best memoriesMontreux cotton-candy sky, Lake Blausee's crystal clear water, sledding in Beatenberg, and a Range Rover upgrade on the rental.
  • Worth itSwitzerland in winter, even at that price.
  • Would changeFewer one- or two-night stops. Road time, trains and luggage made it tiring.
