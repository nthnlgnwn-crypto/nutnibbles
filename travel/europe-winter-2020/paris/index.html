Paris slowed the route down

After Milan, Switzerland, Germany, Amsterdam and Brussels, Paris is where I finally slowed down. The Eiffel Tower in winter light, Pierre Herme macarons, lunch at Kong, and a proper ending.

Eiffel winter

Obvious view, great light.

Yes, the Eiffel Tower is the obvious Paris photo. But winter made it less generic: snow on the branches, low light, end-of-trip mood.

Not much to add. It felt like getting to the end of a long trip.

Food notes

Pierre Herme and Kong, short version for now.

The two Paris meals I remember clearly: Pierre Herme macarons and lunch at Kong. They stay here for now. I need more detail before either gets its own food post.

What's missing: what we ordered, what stood out, whether it was worth the money, and whether I'd call it a must-try or just a nice stop.

City walks

Paris helped the trip feel less rushed.

After all those short stops, the small stuff mattered: courtyards, balcony views, winter walks. And not having to pack up and move again right away.

Final takeaway

Paris was a good ending because I needed a break.

Not because I saw everything, but because the trip finally slowed down. After Switzerland, Germany, Amsterdam and Brussels, I needed that.

Personal take: I'd end in Paris again, but cut some of the earlier stops so I'd arrive less tired.
