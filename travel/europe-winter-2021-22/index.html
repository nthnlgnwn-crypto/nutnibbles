Switzerland / 27 Dec 2021 - 8 Jan 2022

A practical winter route through the Swiss Alps

Zurich, Bern, Zermatt, St Moritz and Poschiavo in winter. Snow views, Velogemel, sunrise in Zermatt, the Glacier Express, and one very clear lesson about booking for New Year.

  • Switzerland

Not a full Switzerland guide. Just what I'd want to remember: beautiful days in the Alps, a few city stops, and a reminder that winter travel gets a lot harder if you're casual about where you sleep and what you carry.

The pretty parts are easy to remember. The lesson is even easier: book Switzerland in winter early, especially around New Year.

Zurich to Bern, then Zermatt, St Moritz and Poschiavo, then back to Zurich. Snowy, pretty, and very Swiss, including the prices.

The best bits were simple: Velogemel in the snow, sunrise in Zermatt, and getting around the Alps by cable car, train and snowy road.

Memory

Velogemel and sunrise were the best bits

Planning Lesson

Don't wing New Year lodging

Our Zermatt hotel got canceled, and I learned: book winter stays early, especially New Year, and think about your luggage before picking a place with stairs.

Zurich + Bern

City stops before the mountains

Zurich and Bern were where we stayed before heading into the mountains. Not full city guides, just what we did: Old Town walks, Christmas markets, the lake and the landmarks, and a few food names.

In Zurich: Old Town, Grossmunster, Lake Zurich, Fraumunster, Illuminarium at the National Museum, and The Nest at Storchen Zurich. In Bern: Bear Pit, Einstein Haus, Kunstmuseum, Berner Munster, Zytglogge, Bundeshaus, and the Christmas market.

Glacier 3000 + Grindelwald First

Snow days start here

Here it stopped being about cities and started being about doing stuff in the snow. Glacier 3000 was the high-up part: the Peak Walk by Tissot, walking on the glacier, the Ice Cathedral, playing in the snow. Grindelwald and First were the fun part: sledging, Velogemel, getting around the mountain.

Zermatt

The sunrise made the whole trip worth it

Zermatt is the picture that stuck: sunrise, snow, and how quiet it is before everyone's out. Also on the list: time in town, the Matterhorn Museum, Gornergrat Railway, Gornergrat Observatory, and maybe Glacier Palace, Sunnegga and Wolli's Park.

Planning Lesson

Book Zermatt early around New Year

The most useful lesson wasn't a view. Our Zermatt hotel got canceled last minute, so we ended up in an Airbnb. It worked, but suddenly the luggage was a real problem.

Next time I'd book New Year lodging way earlier and think properly about luggage. Stairs, snow and heavy bags in a mountain town in winter: not a small thing.

St Moritz + Poschiavo

After Zermatt: trains, roads, quieter places

Glacier Express to St Moritz, then the St Moritz and Poschiavo part of the trip. Short list: Muottas Muragl, snowshoeing, Lake St Moritz, Corviglia and Piz Nair, and Poschiavo as a quieter base.

Food Notes

Places we ate, not reviews (yet)

Just the names for now. Zurich and Bern: Kronenhalle, Zeughauskeller, Haus Hiltl, Babu's Bakery / Cafe Schober, Rooftop Grill, and Rooftop Igloo. Zermatt: After Seven, Ferdinand, and Myoko. St Moritz and Poschiavo: Royal Mongolian Restaurant, Panorama Terrace, Alphutte Clavadatsch, and La Fonte.

No reviews, because what I really remember from this trip is the mountains, Velogemel, the sunrise, and the lodging mess.

Final Takeaway

Worth it, if you plan properly

Compared to the 2020 Europe trip, Europe Winter 2021/22 taught me more about planning. More Switzerland, more mountains, and a good reminder that pretty winter views come with hard-to-find rooms, annoying luggage, and a very real bill.

Don't copy every stop. Keep the mountain highlights, book New Year early, and leave enough slack that the snow days are fun, not just logistics.
