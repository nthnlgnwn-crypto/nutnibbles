South Korea / Seoul / Dec 2022

Seoul framed the trip with winter streets, cafe pauses, and a skyline finish

Seoul was the start and the end. Taller and more orderly than Busan. What I remember: cold light, cafe breaks, Korean barbecue, and the view from the top at the end.

Seoul was two halves: the first days and the last days.

At the start it was winter streets and a calm city mood. At the end it was cafes, food, big indoor spaces, and a skyline view to wrap it up.

Not a full Seoul guide. Just the bits that mattered: how big it is, the cafes, the barbecue, and that last view.

Seoul, short version

First Seoul Impression

Wide roads, winter light, and a slow start

First impression: not a postcard. Cold, wide, orderly. That was fine, because Busan came next and Busan was the warm, relaxed part.

Old streets

Cafe corners and older streets

We did the older parts of Seoul too. Most of my good photos from there have people in them, so this part is words only.

Seoul isn't just malls and skyline views. The quieter streets and cafe stops broke up the big-city stuff. In winter you want those indoor breaks.

Tip: if you go in the cold, plan slow cafe time. The big sightseeing days are easier with it.
