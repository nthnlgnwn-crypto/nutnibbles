South Korea / 8-19 Dec 2022

Seoul city bookends, Busan sea air, and winter food warmth

A compact Korea winter route split between Seoul's city scale and Busan's coastal calm, kept public here as a privacy-clean travel memory rather than a raw itinerary.

This section is useful if you want a simple two-city Korea reference: start and finish in Seoul for city texture, place Busan in the middle for coast and food, and keep the pace flexible enough for winter weather.

This Korea trip works best as a two-city winter rhythm: Seoul for the frame, Busan for the release.

The private itinerary confirms the broad route and date range, but the public version stays intentionally lighter: no hotel details, no flight logistics, and no companion-heavy photos. What matters here is the travel rhythm.

Seoul gave the trip structure with winter streets, cafe corners, Korean barbecue, and skyline views. Busan softened the middle with coastal air, colorful hillside lanes, warm meals, and an easier pace by the water.
