South Korea / 8–19 Dec 2022

Seoul city bookends, Busan sea air, and winter food warmth

A winter trip split between big-city Seoul and Busan by the sea.

  • South Korea
  • 8–19 Dec 2022
  • Seoul -> Busan -> Seoul
  • Winter city + coast
  • Travel notes

Easy two-city plan: start and finish in Seoul, put Busan in the middle for the coast and the food. Leave room for winter weather.

Seoul at both ends, Busan in the middle to slow down.

Seoul was winter streets, cafe corners, Korean barbecue and skyline views.

Busan was the easy middle: sea air, colorful hillside lanes, warm meals, and slow days by the water.
