Busan was easier than Seoul. Sea views, colorful hillsides, warm bowls, and cafe breaks that made the cold easier.

Busan, short version

First impression

Everything relaxed once the water showed up

Busan felt different from Seoul almost straight away. Still busy, but the sea slowed it down. Less formal, more open, and I didn't need to plan every minute.

Gamcheon and the hillsides

Stairs, pastel walls, and a bit of fun

The hillside streets have the most personality. Narrow paths, colorful walls, lots of up and down. Plenty to look at, no big landmark needed.

Coastal Rail / Sea Views

The sea kept showing up between stops

The coastal rail and the sea views are why Busan works in the middle. A break for the eyes after Seoul's packed streets, before heading back north.

Cafe Pause

Sitting down between coastal stops

The cafe wasn't a sight, and it didn't need to be. Sit inside, slow down, then go find the next warm meal.
