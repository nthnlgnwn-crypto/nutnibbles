Opening

Two short Bangkok visits, kept loose

May and June were separate trips, but they felt similar enough to lump together. Not much sightseeing. Just Wat Arun, food, massages, the river, and Bangkok at the pace it works best at: not over-scheduled.

That’s also why the food part is vague. I’d rather not pretend I remember names and dishes I don’t.

Wat Arun

Temple details worth slowing down for

Wat Arun was the big one. What stuck with me was the detail: the layered surface and the bright pattern. Plus Bangkok gives you a huge landmark without some complicated route to get there.

Food stops

Good food, not many names

Food was one of the best parts of these visits, but I didn’t label the casual stops properly in my notes. So for now it’s just photos of eating around Bangkok. Dish and restaurant names can come later, once I’ve checked them.

How I ate

Small meals in between everything

Short, warm days, with small meals squeezed in between temples, massages and the river.

About the names

No guessing

Until I’ve checked the restaurant and dish names, this is a travel note, not a food guide. Better than making stuff up.

City pauses

Massages, cafe corners, no rushing

Massages were a regular thing, but I don’t have the place name yet. Main point: Bangkok felt better with gaps between eating, temples and wherever was next. It was best when I didn’t rush.

ICONSIAM

Evening by the river, a bit calmer

ICONSIAM is the one riverside spot I can name for sure. Evening light, the water, a big city all around, and a quieter end after a day of eating and moving around.

Worth it

Khaan was the dinner that stood out

The most “worth it” thing in Bangkok was Khaan. The menu, dishes and value need their own post, so no full review here. Short version: it was the fine dining that stood out.

Maybe its own post later

If I write up the whole dinner, Khaan gets its own review: menu, favorite dishes, weakest moments, service, value, who should go.

Worth it / would change

What I’d do exactly the same

Wat Arun, ICONSIAM by the river, the casual food, the massages, Khaan. Nothing major I’d skip. Bangkok was good because I kept it simple, not because I packed the days.

Final takeaway

Short Bangkok notes, not a full guide

What I’d take from Bangkok 2025: make time for Wat Arun, leave room for food and massages, keep one evening for the river, and save Khaan for a proper dinner. The rest can stay loose.
