United Kingdom / 7-22 Nov 2023

London first, then a slow climb into Scotland.

This is the UK and Scotland trip as a photo-led journal: London easing us in with park water and theatre lights, an in-between England road stretch, then a colder, moodier run through Glasgow, the Highlands, whisky rooms, Edinburgh, and a final return to London lights.

Route

London to Edinburgh, with a moody northern middle.

The trip begins gently in London, moves through Windsor and Manchester, follows the road toward Scotland, and ends with Edinburgh stone before returning south to winter lights.

Vibe

Autumn streets, warm plates, grey skies.

Less checklist travel, more little scenes: a quiet London lake, casual food stops, rainy Highland roads, whisky rooms, old stone, and lights that kept the colder days from feeling too heavy.

London

First city rhythm

Park water, theatre lights, market bread, and easy comfort-food energy before the route moved north.

England stops

Castles, meals, and small detours

Windsor brought the classic stone mood, while Manchester and Blackpool became the useful middle: casual plates, sweets, errands, and small detours.

Scotland

The road got moodier

Autumn paths, grey Highland roads, stormy views, whisky rooms, and stone monuments gave this chapter the strongest atmosphere.

Edinburgh

Old stone and winter lights

Edinburgh closed the trip with castle gates, palace stone, old rooms, and one final return to London winter brightness.
