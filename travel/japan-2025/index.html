Japan / 18–29 Jan 2025

Winter, family time, and food that never went wrong.

Family trip with my parents and younger brother. It was fun, mostly because I’ve always loved winter and snow. And in Japan the food is almost always a good call.

Route

Sapporo, Osaka, Kyoto, Tokyo

Snow first, then Kansai food and city walks, a kimono day in Kyoto, and Tokyo to finish.

Vibe

Trip notes, plus food reviews

Split by city. Each one has the sights, what we ate, photos, and what I actually thought.

Where to Start

Snow first, then food.

Go in order: Sapporo for snow and Hokkaido food, Osaka for the big city, Kyoto for one family day, then Tokyo for the last hotel, Fuji, and more food.

01 / Snow start

Begin in Sapporo

Open space, winter parks, ramen, seafood. The calmest city of the trip.

 02 / Kansai middle

Move into Osaka

Comfort food, shopping, night gardens, and warmer than Hokkaido.

 03 / The finish

End with Tokyo

The one worth bookmarking: Cerulean hotel notes, the Fuji day trip, Cat Lounge MOCHA, Meiji Jingu, and gyukatsu.
Favorite Snow Stop

Moerenuma Park

Open snow, the glass pyramid, families playing everywhere. Calm, and I still remember it.

Best Food Start

Arrival night ramen

Warm, savoury first bowl at 22:30, right after landing in Sapporo. Good way to start.

Osaka Moment

Night garden lights

Osaka Botanic Garden was a quiet night out after Dotonbori and all the shopping.

Kyoto Side Trip

Rented kimonos

The whole family dressed up for the day, which made the old streets, shrine gates and snack stalls extra fun to remember.
