Japan Winter 2025 / Stop two: Kansai

Osaka food walks, bright signs, and softer night detours

Out of the Hokkaido snow and into somewhere louder and warmer: a really good late lunch, a quick walk around Dotonbori, a light garden at night, family time at Osaka Castle, and a last day that went from a quiet temple to loud, colorful Shinsekai.

Louder and warmer: Dotonbori walks, comfort food, night gardens, city views, temples, and colorful Shinsekai.

After Sapporo, Osaka was a switch straight away. Less still, more lit up. But it had enough quiet corners that it wasn't all noise.

We flew from Sapporo to Osaka on 22 Jan, and it changed fast. From calm, snowy Hokkaido to bright signs, later meals, busier streets, and a city that was more fun right away.

The mix is what worked. Osaka had Dotonbori and Shinsekai, but also a night garden, a quiet temple, and a second visit to Osaka Castle that meant more because I was there with family.

Quick Version

  • Best mixBright Dotonbori, then a calm night walk at Osaka Botanic Garden.
  • Best first mealRyunosu near Shinsaibashi. Rich meat and comforting udon.
  • Most Osaka stopShinsekai. Loud, colorful, fun, not subtle at all.
  • Good forPeople who want comfort food and a busier city, plus a few quiet moments.

Me, on Osaka

22 Jan / Arrival, late lunch, and a calmer night

Sapporo snow to bright Osaka in one day

On 22 Jan we flew from Sapporo to Osaka. Hokkaido snow one minute, busy city the next: late lunch near Shinsaibashi, a short Dotonbori stroll, Pokemon Center, and a calm night walk at Osaka Botanic Garden.

That first day sums up Osaka pretty well. Commercial and bright, then suddenly quiet somewhere else at night.

We didn't try to do too much after the flight. Small stops, all different: a great first meal, a little city walk, then a slow evening.

Day mood

Busy when we got in, calm by the time we were at the garden.

Good for

An easy first Osaka day that still shows you what the city's like.

22 Jan / Ryunosu near Shinsaibashi

Late lunch, strong start

After flying in from Sapporo, we had a late lunch at Ryunosu near Shinsaibashi. The meat was really satisfying: rich and filling, the kind of meal that makes you feel settled on arrival day. The udon was a big portion and was the comfort-food part.

Food note: very satisfying first Osaka meal, especially after the flight. The meat was the best part. The udon made it feel like more of a real meal.

Read the Ryunosu lunch note

22 Jan / Dotonbori and Pokemon Center

Quick city walk before evening

After lunch we walked around Dotonbori for a bit and went to Pokemon Center. Light, easy walk: bright signs, the canal, lots of shopping.

22 Jan / Osaka Botanic Garden

Night garden full of lights

At night we went to Osaka Botanic Garden. Beautiful and calm, with lit-up trees, glowing installations, and a quiet walking route. A completely different Osaka.

My take: worth it if you want an Osaka night that isn't only neon and food streets. Quiet, beautiful, and a calm end to a busy day.

23 Jan / Castle, curry, skyline, and shrine

Places I'd seen before, different with family

23 Jan was a family-pace day: Osaka Castle in the daytime, curry near Shin Umeda, up the Umeda Sky Building, and Namba Yasaka Shrine in the evening.

My second time at Osaka Castle, but this time with my parents and younger brother. Same place, but with family the walk, the views and the little museum bits felt more personal.

So it was less about seeing something new and more about going back with different people.

My take

Been before? Osaka Castle is still worth going back to with different people.

Lunch

After Osaka Castle we had lunch at CoCo Ichibanya around Shin Umeda. Simple, filling Japanese curry, which made sense before going up to the observatory.

Read the CoCo Curry lunch note

23 Jan / Umeda Sky Building

Big city views after lunch

After lunch we went up Umeda Sky Building. The observatory is called Kuchu Teien Observatory, also known as the Floating Garden Observatory. With the open structure and the long escalator, you know where you are before you even get to the view.

My take: good Osaka skyline stop, especially if you like observatories and weird buildings.

23 Jan / Namba Yasaka Shrine

Evening stop with one giant lion head

In the evening we went to Namba Yasaka Shrine. The huge lion-head stage makes it completely different from the quieter temples. A great last stop for the day.

My take: a short visit is enough, but the lion-head shrine sticks with you and it's very photogenic.

24 Jan / Namba morning, temple calm, and Shinsekai ending

Last Osaka day: easy, then loud again

On 24 Jan we kept the morning easy with a walk around Dotonbori and Namba, then a quiet temple, then ended in loud, colorful Shinsekai.

24 Jan / Shitennoji Temple

Simple, quiet temple

At around 11am we went to Shitennoji Temple. Not the biggest temple of the trip, but quiet, with red buildings and small details. Simple and beautiful.

My take: small but beautiful. Good for a quiet Osaka moment between the busy food streets and loud neighborhoods.

24 Jan / Shinsekai

Colorful afternoon to finish Osaka

After lunch we went to Shinsekai around 2pm. Loud, colorful, very Osaka, with Tsutenkaku in the background and giant signs everywhere.

My take: Shinsekai is about the vibe, not calm sightseeing. Busy and fun, and that's the point.
