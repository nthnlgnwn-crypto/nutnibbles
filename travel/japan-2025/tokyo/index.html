Japan Winter 2025 / Last city

Tokyo stays, food, Fuji, and soft pauses

Tokyo was the last stop, and it pulled the whole trip together. One hotel that really overdelivered, a late lunch worth remembering, a Fuji-san day where we had to work for the view, and small breaks for shrine paths, cats, Harajuku and donuts.

Short version: where we stayed, the meal that mattered most, and the slow detours that were worth it.

Tokyo was the last big breath of the trip. Still busy, but with enough comfort, food and quiet breaks that the ending didn’t feel rushed.

On 26 Jan we flew into Tokyo from Kansai. After snowy Sapporo, loud Osaka and the Kyoto kimono day, Tokyo was the all-rounder: good meals, wandering around neighborhoods, a comfy hotel, and little stops that made the last city feel like a proper finish.

What worked was the balance. Still big-city energy, but also slower moments: a hotel that felt like a proper reset, one meal good enough for its own food note, and a few easy detours before the trip ended.

Me, on Tokyo

Tokyo Stay / Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel

Best hotel of the trip

In Tokyo we stayed at Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel, and it was the best hotel of the whole Japan trip. It’s on the older side, but that didn’t matter. Big room, really useful facilities, and a comfy base for the last city.

The gym stood out most. Properly equipped, not a token hotel corner. After several cities of moving around, that mattered more than a newer-looking room would have.

The extra hotel photos are from the official Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel site.

Personal take: older hotel, but big rooms and good facilities. If you want comfort at the end of a busy Japan trip, this was the best stay we had.

Stay Note

Best for: finishing a busy trip with space, comfort and facilities you’ll use.

26 Jan / Late Lunch / Gyukatsu Motomura

Tokyo started with the best gyukatsu

After landing we had a late lunch at Gyukatsu Motomura. Exactly the first meal I want after changing cities: simple, filling, memorable, no big plan needed. Crispy beef cutlet, small sides, rice bowls, and a hot stone that keeps you busy.

Why It Works

Rich, warm, and hits the spot right after a travel day.

Best Detail

The hot stone on the table lets you finish each bite how you like it.

NutNibbles Take

Best gyukatsu of the trip. The first Tokyo meal I’d send people to.

27 Jan / Fuji-san 1 Day Tour

Picture hunting around Mt. Fuji

On 27 Jan we joined a one-day tour to hunt for Fuji-san pictures. Cloudy weather, clouds moving around the mountain, so no guaranteed postcard. Mostly waiting for a gap.

Even with the peak hiding, Fuji-san looked dramatic. Snow, dark clouds, village rooftops, lake reflections, sudden clear moments. Every stop showed a slightly different mountain.

Personal take: fun photo day. The clouds made it less predictable, but every clear view felt earned. Treat it as a scenic day out. The perfect Fuji shot is a bonus.

Best for

People who enjoy the day itself, not just the postcard shot.

Trip mood

Cloudy, pretty, a bit suspenseful. Kept every stop interesting.

28 Jan / Shibuya Cat Lounge MOCHA

Cat break before heading back out

On 28 Jan we went to Shibuya Cat Lounge MOCHA. After the big sightseeing days it was a calm indoor break: warm lights, lots of wood, manga shelves, and cats acting like they owned the place.

What stood out

Feeding time pulled the whole room into one calm little circle. No chaotic tourist crowd.

NutNibbles take

Cute and easy. Good Shibuya stop when we wanted something slower between plans.

28 Jan / Meiji Jingu, Harajuku, and I'm donut?

Shrine walk, then back to the city

Later on 28 Jan we went from cats to a walk around Meiji Jingu. Peaceful and roomy, with sake barrels, tall trees and wooden ema prayers. Quiet enough that the city felt much farther away than it was.

The ema area is what I remember most. Lots of small wishes hanging under the sacred tree, ours included.

Then Harajuku and Omotesando brought the city back: shiny buildings, sunset, and a snack mission at I’m donut?. Tokyo walks are better with a snack goal.

Snack note: I’m donut? had a fun menu and a beautiful display. Sweet custard ones, plus savory ones like prosciutto and teriyaki egg.

Best detail

Quiet shrine first made Harajuku and Omotesando hit harder. Better than one flat pace all day.
