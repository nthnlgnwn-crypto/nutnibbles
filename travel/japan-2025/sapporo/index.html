Japan Winter 2025 / Stop one: Hokkaido

Sapporo snow days, warm food stops, and a calm winter start

First stop, and the calmest one: ramen the night we landed, walks through parks buried in snow, a really good seafood lunch, a shrine, the TV tower, and a last-day tour that went from zoo animals to cabins in snowy woods.

  • Sapporo
  • 18–21 Jan 2025
  • First stop
  • Snow + sightseeing
  • Family trip

Snowy parks, seafood lunches, ramen nights, winter views. A calm Hokkaido start to the family trip.

Sapporo was a good first stop. The food was comforting, the snow did most of the work, and even the busy spots were slower than the cities after it.

We got in late on 18 Jan and went for ramen near the hotel. After that: snowy parks, seafood lunches, a mountain viewpoint, a shrine, a sweets factory, and a one-day winter tour that packed a lot into the last day.

It's not just the snow. Sapporo let us ease in with warm meals, open space and a slower pace before Osaka, Kyoto and Tokyo got busier.

Me, on Sapporo

18 Jan / Ramen the night we landed

Late first bowl, good first bowl

We landed at New Chitose Airport on the night of 18 Jan, checked in, and went straight to the ramen alley near the hotel. It was already around 22:30, and somehow that made the first bowl in Sapporo taste even better.

The alley was narrow, bright and busy, with tiny ramen shops packed side by side. Our shop was small and cramped. Very Japan: compact, warm, lots going on.

Food note: savoury, comforting broth. Exactly what I wanted after the flight.

19 Jan / Moerenuma Park, seafood lunch, Mt. Moiwa, and hotel-room dinner

The busiest Sapporo day, and it still felt easy

19 Jan had a bit of everything: open snow in the morning, seafood after the cold, a mountain viewpoint later, then a convenience-store dinner back at the hotel.

Moerenuma Park

Snow, families, and the glass pyramid

My second time at Moerenuma Park, and it was still beautiful. Cold, but the glass pyramid looks great against the snow, and the whole park is calm in a way I love.

We walked through the snowy park and saw families playing around. That part was heartwarming. People were really out enjoying winter together.

My take: still worth it, even the second time. Glass pyramid, open snow, families everywhere. Peaceful, and a good easy first full day.

Seafood lunch

After Moerenuma Park we had lunch at a place whose name I forgot. The food I remember. The salmon sashimi was fresh, and the king crab leg was juicy. Proper Hokkaido.

Read the seafood lunch note

Mt. Moiwa

Snowy summit, then Sapporo at night

After lunch we went up Mt. Moiwa. Snow on the tracks, bare trees, and Sapporo off in the distance.

The view changed a lot toward evening. In the afternoon everything was pale and snowy. Later the city lights came on, and that was the best part.

My take: a great Sapporo viewpoint. Stay long enough to see it go from daylight to night lights.

7-Eleven hotel dinner

After a full day, dinner was a 7-Eleven haul back at the hotel. Simple, easy, and part of the Japan experience too.

Food note: crab-style snacks, egg, grilled chicken, cheese chikuwa, and a chocolate cream puff. Less backup dinner, more hotel-room tasting session.

20 Jan / Odori, shrine, lunch, and sweets

City day, with a shrine in the middle

20 Jan started in the center with Odori Park and Sapporo TV Tower, then Hokkaido Jingu Shrine, lunch nearby, and Shiroi Koibito Park for sweets at the end.

Odori Park and Sapporo TV Tower

Snowy city walk around the red tower

We started 20 Jan around Odori Park and Sapporo TV Tower. Classic Sapporo morning: snow on the ground, the red tower over the park, and the sky getting brighter.

From the tower, Odori Park looked like a long snowy line cutting through the city. Down on the street there was winter stuff everywhere: a big Christmas tree, a bakery, fair-style displays.

My take: easy, must-do Sapporo stop. Central, simple, and you get both the street view and the view from up high.

Hokkaido Jingu Shrine

Quieter shrine stop after the tower

After Odori Park and Sapporo TV Tower we went to Hokkaido Jingu Shrine. Snow on the approach, a wooden shrine building. Slower and calmer.

My take: a nice change after the city center. Peaceful and traditional. Fits a winter day in Sapporo.

Lunch nearby

After Hokkaido Jingu we ate nearby. Comforting, easy plate: omurice, fried seafood, salad, and a hamburger-style patty with sauce.

Read the lunch note

Shiroi Koibito Park

Sweets factory to end the day

Shiroi Koibito Park was a lighter stop after shrines and city views. You get to see how the famous biscuit is made, so it's more than just sweets shopping. The winter decorations outside were cute, even with the crowd.

Outside it felt like a winter theme park. The factory viewing area made it more interesting than just buying souvenirs. Busy, but still charming, that very Japan thing where everything is neatly presented.

My take: worth going once. I wouldn't repeat it every trip, but it's a fun, photogenic stop for a family winter trip.

21 Jan / One-day winter tour

Animals, snow riding, and cabin lights in one long day

On 21 Jan we did a one-day tour: Asahiyama Zoo first, then a snow ATV ride in Biei, a very crowded pass-by at the Furano Christmas Tree, and finally Ningle Terrace in the snowy woods.

Asahiyama Zoo

Winter animals and the famous penguin walk

The zoo in winter was very Hokkaido: bright snow, cold air, and animals that looked totally at home.

Best part for me: seeing tanuki for the first time. So cute and fluffy. Easily the part of the zoo I remember most. We saw other winter animals around the snowy enclosures too, which made it very different from a normal zoo day.

The penguin walk is iconic and it's what Asahiyama Zoo is known for, but the crowd was intense. Lots of people queueing and watching. A bit painful, even though the penguins themselves were fun.

My take: worth it in winter, mainly for the animals in the snow. If you care about a good view of the penguin walk, be ready for the crowd.

Biei Snow ATV

Snow ATVs in Biei

Second stop on the tour was a snow ATV ride in Biei. More active after the zoo: open snow fields, clear blue sky, and the whole family in helmets.

It was fun, mostly because the winter scenery made a simple ride feel special. The one downside was the gas and fuel smell. Noticeable, but it didn't ruin anything.

My take: fun once. The snow was the main thing, and it was a good family stop after the zoo.

Furano Christmas Tree

Third stop was the Furano Christmas Tree, but it was so crowded I didn't get off the bus. So, more of a drive-by.

My take: probably nice if the crowd is manageable. That day it wasn't worth pushing through just for a photo.

Ningle Terrace warning

The walk from the bus stop to the main spot is quite far and slippery. We had to be really careful on the snow and ice.

Ningle Terrace

Cabins hidden in the snowy woods

Once we got there, it was beautiful. Small wooden cabins, soft lights, trees and snow. Like a little winter village in the forest.

My take: beautiful and worth seeing, but wear shoes with good grip and walk slowly. The slippery path is the main warning here.
