Japan Winter 2025 / Kyoto side trip

A Kyoto kimono day with family photos and small street details

Kyoto was only one day, but it stuck because it had a theme: rented kimonos, old streets, family photos, shrine gates, and snacks. Short trip, didn’t feel rushed.

  • Kyoto
  • 25 Jan 2025
  • One-day side trip
  • Kimono + shrine walk
  • Family trip

One day in Kyoto: kimono rental, family photos, shrine gates and street snacks. No big sightseeing checklist.

Kyoto felt lighter than the other cities, but not smaller. The kimonos set the mood for the whole day, so even simple walks and family photos felt like a thing.

On 25 Jan we did a one-day trip to Kyoto and rented kimonos. The joke was that we’d blend in with the old streets. The best part was just seeing the whole family dressed up and walking around together.

We kept it simple on purpose. The clothes, the shrine and a few snacks were plenty. No need for a lot of stops.

Me, on Kyoto

25 Jan / Kyoto day trip

Kimonos on, old streets, done

The kimonos made the day a bit fun and a bit formal. Even simple family photos outside wooden buildings or temple gates felt special because everyone was dressed for it.

No huge itinerary needed. The clothes, streets and shrine made every stop feel like part of the same day.

Personal take: one of the most fun family-photo days of the trip. Touristy, yes, but the good kind, and very different from the Osaka city walks.
