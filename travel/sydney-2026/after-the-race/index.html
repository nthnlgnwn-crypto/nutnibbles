Sydney, after the race

Five days of the city you actually get to enjoy once your legs stop mattering: a recovery dinner that underdelivered, a library that overdelivered, a beach walk, a musical, a zoo, and the best meal of the trip.

  • Sydney
  • Bondi & Tamarama
  • Taronga Zoo
  • Sydney Fish Market
  • Food-heavy

The race was the reason for the trip. This chapter is the part I would repeat. It is also where every good meal happened, which is not a coincidence — you eat differently when nothing depends on your legs tomorrow.

Sydney is a much better city when you are not saving your legs for something.

The first day after was mostly a slow shuffle between coffee and food, and a return to the Opera House to take the photograph I had been too destroyed to take the day before.

After that the days find a rhythm: a coffee, one proper thing, one long walk, one dinner. Three things in a day turned out to be the correct number of things when you have already been in a city for a week.

At a Glance

  • Best mealSydney Fish Market on 4 September. Sea urchin and oysters, eaten standing up.
  • Best sit-downDOPA. Beef that was properly juicy and tender, and condiments doing real work.
  • Best eveningSaga Bar. Hidden-bar quality that usually gets oversold and here actually holds up.
  • What didn’t workDoo Dee Paidang. Decent Thai food that lost to my own expectations on the walk over.

Sydney Fish Market, 4 September

31 Aug

High expectations, medium Thai food, and then a very good bar

The morning was coffee and toasties and moving slowly. The afternoon was going back to the Opera House to take the photograph. The evening was the one genuine disappointment of the week, followed by the best hour of it.

1 Sep

Coffee, a library I didn’t expect to like, and the Opera House

Started at Stitch Coffee — cold brew, a piccolo, egg tarts. Then Green Square Library, which is the surprise of the trip. Up the Sydney Tower Eye, lunch at the Fortune Alley food court, dessert at Freo, then the harbour.

2 Sep

A beach day, then a musical, in the same twelve hours

Coffee at Eksentrik, then the bus to Bondi. Saw the beach and the Icebergs from a distance rather than up close — a decision I would defend, since the walk along to Tamarama is the better part anyway.

3 Sep

A short day, on purpose

Bohaus Coffee just before one, Taronga Zoo at half two, dinner at MuMu at six. Three things in a day is the correct number of things when you have already been in a city for a week.

4 Sep

The best meal of the trip, and it wasn’t a restaurant

Sydney Fish Market, which is where the trip peaked. Dim sum at TinTin first, then the actual point: oysters, sea urchin, caviar, salmon sashimi, prawns.

Best for

Anyone who would rather stand up and eat something excellent than sit down and eat something impressive. Go hungry and bring cash-level decisiveness; the counters move fast.

What didn’t work

Nothing at the market, and I want to be specific about why I am confident: I ate five separate things across two hours and the only thing I would change is having eaten the dim sum first and arrived less hungry.
