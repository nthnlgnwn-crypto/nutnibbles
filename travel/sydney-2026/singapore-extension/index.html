Singapore / 5–8 Sep 2026

The Singapore extension

A volcano in the Sunda Strait ended the trip, and then didn’t. Four extra days of being stuck in Singapore, and what I did with them.

  • Singapore
  • 5–8 Sep 2026
  • Unplanned
  • Krakatau
  • Four days

Two free days in Singapore: great story. From day three, on my own money: just a bill. Both happened on the same trip, about forty-eight hours apart.

The flight home went through Singapore. The Singapore–Jakarta leg got cancelled: volcanic ash from Krakatau.

Singapore Airlines put us up at the Royal Plaza on Scotts, with breakfast, lunch and dinner buffets included. For two days this was a gift. Someone else’s hotel, someone else’s meals, and a city I like anyway.

Then the coverage ran out.

The Singapore extension

5 Sep

A volcano in the Sunda Strait ended the trip, and then didn’t

Out of Sydney in the evening, into Singapore, and straight into the news that the last leg wasn’t happening. The airline handled it well: a hotel on Scotts Road with all three meals included. For two days, a gift.

6 Sep

Checked out at nine, checked back in by lunchtime

Out of the hotel around nine for Changi, rebooked on a flight around noon. Then: Soekarno-Hatta still closed for the day. Checked back in and made a day out of a day that wasn’t supposed to exist.

7–8 Sep

The part where it stops being a gift

The airline’s coverage ran out. The last two days were on my own money, still stuck in Singapore, waiting for an airport in another country to reopen.

Three rebookings and a Tuesday afternoon

First the flight home got pushed all the way to the 12th. At that point it felt less like a delay and more like moving here. Then it moved up to the 9th. Then Soekarno-Hatta reopened on the 8th, and Singapore Airlines put me on the 13:10 that afternoon. Home on Tuesday 8 September: four days later than the ticket said, four days earlier than I’d made peace with.

If it happens to you

Take the airline hotel and the buffet, no guilt, and use the free days properly. They’re the only part you’ll remember fondly. Then start counting, because the coverage ends before the chaos does. And don’t plan around the rebooked date. Mine moved three times in three days.

Honest downside

A trip extension you didn’t choose is not a bonus holiday. Two days of it is a story. After that it’s a hotel bill, a laundry problem, and checking every day whether an airport in another country has reopened.
