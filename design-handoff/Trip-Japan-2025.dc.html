If you’re planning the same route

Sapporo first was the right call. Starting in the coldest place meant the trip warmed up rather than fizzled out, and the snow stopped feeling novel by the time we reached Kansai anyway.

Twelve days for four cities is comfortable rather than tight, as long as you accept that Kyoto becomes a day trip rather than a base. Tokyo last gives you the easiest airport exit and the best hotel comfort at the point you need it most.

Practical
Go when
Late January for snow; expect short daylight
