Kindling made a birthday dinner feel intimate instead of overly polished.

A Jakarta fine dining reference that feels more homey than theatrical.

The crab custard was the clear favourite: delicate, comforting, exactly right for a birthday.

Value read
Expensive, but worth it for the full special-occasion treatment.

Kindling feels special because it does not chase a cold fine-dining mood. It feels intimate, slightly jadul in a good way.

We moved through three rooms across the night: one for the appetizers, one for the mains, one for dessert. That small change in setting made the dinner feel more like a real occasion.

Weak spot

The firefly squid read too fishy for me. Beautifully plated, but it didn’t land.
