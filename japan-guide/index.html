Some guides try to cover everything. This one should become more selective: what helped, what was worth it, what I would adjust, and which meals or city bases deserve a real note.

The current Japan pages already hold the raw material: Sapporo snow, Osaka comfort stops, a Kyoto kimono day, Tokyo hotel and food notes, plus casual meal pages that are easier to trust because they came from the actual route.

The future guide may become a PDF, a Google Maps list, a tighter planning page, or a mix of all three. For now, this page is a soft preview and interest marker.
