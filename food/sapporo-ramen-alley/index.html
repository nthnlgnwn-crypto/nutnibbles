Food Diary / Sapporo / 18 Jan 2025 / 22:30

Arrival-night ramen in a tiny, busy alley.

After landing at New Chitose Airport and checking in, this late ramen stop felt exactly right: small, cramped, warm, and full of first-night Sapporo energy.

  • Sapporo
  • Late dinner
  • Ramen alley
  • First night
  • Japan Winter 2025

The broth was the part that stayed with me: savoury, comforting, and exactly what I wanted after a long travel day.

The first Sapporo meal did not need to be polished. It needed to be hot, savoury, and comforting.

The space was small and cramped, but in a way that felt very Japan: close, efficient, and full of life. If you want a relaxed dinner with room to linger, this is probably not it. As an arrival-night stop, though, the tight counter feeling actually helped.

The broth was the highlight. It was very nice, savoury, and comforting at that hour, which made the whole meal feel like a proper start to Hokkaido.

At a Glance

  • Best forA first-night ramen stop when warmth, speed, and atmosphere matter more than space.
  • What stood outThe savoury broth and the cramped little alley atmosphere.
  • Room moodBusy, close, and very alive.
  • Worth it?Yes, especially as the first Sapporo food memory.

Quick Take

A travel-day bowl that delivered exactly what was needed

The best thing about this stop is that it makes sense in context. After the airport and check-in, the bowl felt like comfort first and sightseeing detail second.

The shop was not spacious, and I would not position it as a slow or elegant meal. But the small counter-style setup added to the experience rather than hurting it, especially because this was a late arrival-night dinner.

Trip context: exactly the kind of first-night Sapporo stop that lets the city start tasting real right away.

Best Bite

The broth. It carried the whole late-night bowl.

Not for

Anyone looking for a spacious or slow dinner setting.
