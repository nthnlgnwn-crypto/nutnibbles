Food Diary / Sapporo / 19 Jan 2025

Fresh salmon sashimi and a juicy king crab leg.

I forgot the restaurant name, but not the meal. After Moerenuma Park, this was exactly the kind of Hokkaido seafood lunch that made sense: fresh, simple, and very easy to enjoy.

Sometimes the name fades first, but the taste stays. This was one of those meals.

The place name may be gone from memory, but the lunch still holds because the seafood was simple and good.

The salmon sashimi tasted very fresh, with that clean, rich flavor that makes a bowl feel special even when the setup is straightforward. It was the kind of meal that did not need much explanation once it hit the table.

The king crab leg was also a highlight: juicy, satisfying, and fun to eat. That combination made the lunch feel worth keeping in the trip record even without the restaurant name attached to it anymore.
