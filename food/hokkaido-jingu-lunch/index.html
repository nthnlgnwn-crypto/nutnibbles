Food Diary / Sapporo / 20 Jan 2025

A comforting lunch near Hokkaido Jingu.

After visiting the shrine, lunch nearby was exactly the kind of practical travel-day meal that keeps the route moving: warm, familiar, and filling without asking for too much planning.

  • Sapporo

  • Near Hokkaido Jingu

Omurice, fried seafood, salad, and a hamburger-style patty with sauce made it feel more like a family-trip reset than a destination restaurant stop.

Not every useful food stop needs a strong point of view. Sometimes the best thing is that it simply fits the day.

This was the kind of lunch that works well during a travel day. Nothing too heavy or complicated, but warm, familiar, and filling enough before continuing to the next place.

The mixed plate made it feel like a comfortable family-trip lunch rather than a meal you build a route around, and that is exactly why I wanted to keep it in the journal.
