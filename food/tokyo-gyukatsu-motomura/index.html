Food Diary / Tokyo / 26 Jan 2025

Gyukatsu Motomura made Tokyo start very strong.

After flying from Kansai to Tokyo, this late lunch did exactly what a first Tokyo meal should do: it felt warm, rich, fun, and memorable from the first bite.

  • Tokyo
  • Late lunch
  • Gyukatsu Motomura
  • 26 Jan 2025
  • Japan Winter 2025

Crispy beef cutlet, hot stone finishing, little side dishes, and one of the strongest food memories from the trip.

Some first meals just do the job. This one immediately felt like one of the best food stops of the trip.

The beef cutlet was crispy outside and tender inside, and the small hot stone made the meal more fun because each slice could be finished bite by bite. That little interaction made it feel more memorable than a normal fried-cutlet lunch.

The rice bowls, cabbage, pickles, sauces, and side dishes made the whole set feel complete. It was not just a quick lunch after a flight; it became the meal that made Tokyo start strong.

Quick Take

Worth the hype because the whole set feels thought through

This is not just about one crispy cutlet. The full tray matters: sauces, cabbage, rice, small side dishes, and the fun of cooking each slice a little more on the stone.

What made the lunch memorable was how easy it was to enjoy every part of it. The beef delivered immediately, but the supporting pieces made it feel generous instead of one-note.

Trip context: exactly the right late lunch after landing in Tokyo, when you want something that resets your energy and gives the city a strong start.

Best Bite

The cutlet itself, especially when each slice gets a final moment on the hot stone.

Best for

Travel-day lunches that still feel like a real destination meal.
