Why I keep going back

Personal Review

Modern Indonesian tasting menu. Warm, not stiff

When someone asks me for a fine dining spot in Jakarta that’s impressive but not cold, August is an easy answer. The room is intimate and homey. The pacing leaves time to actually talk. The service is hospitable, not overly formal.

This was a date night, and that’s where August works best. Polished, not intimidating. Expensive, yes, but it makes sense once the set menu gets going and every course feels thought through.

Teh Kotak is the one I kept thinking about after. Playful, familiar, very August: fancy, but still tasting like home. The BTM Noodle was comfort food, just sharper and more dressed up. The striploin was the big main-course moment you want from a special dinner.

Worth it? Yes. For a birthday, anniversary or a date you want to get right in Jakarta, August still belongs near the top of the list. It gets both the food and the mood right.

Disclosure: I paid for this meal in full. Nothing here was hosted, comped, or discounted.
